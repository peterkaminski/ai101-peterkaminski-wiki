## Top Large Language Models (LLMs)

- Claude 3.7 Sonnet - Pete's favorite
- ChatGPT 4o - also good
- Perplexity - many people like the search-based knowledge
- Pi - supposedly the AI with the most "emotional intelligence"
- Copilot - kind of like ChatGPT, but with a Microsoft overlay
- the open (ish) ones; I (Pete) am out of date on the current best, so none listed here

## Top Image Generators

Midjourney is one of the hardest to control, but still makes some of the most beautiful images.

DALL-E has been replaced with ChatGPT 4o image generation, which makes good images and is probably the easiest to control.

There are now a number of other great ones, let Pete know which are your favorites and why.
